  • Kilojoules vs calories: how to read an Australian nutrition label

    One Calorie is 4.184 kJ, so divide kilojoules by about 4.2 to get Calories. Use the per 100 g column to compare foods and the per serve column, adjusted to your portion, for what you actually eat.

    19 November 2026

  • How much protein do you need? A guide for Australians

    The official Australian minimum is 0.75 g per kg of body weight for women and 0.84 g for men. If you train or are losing weight, research supports about 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg.

    18 November 2026
