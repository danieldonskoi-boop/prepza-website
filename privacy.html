Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Prepza helps you plan meals and track what you eat. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information you give us

Account details: your email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider - we never see it), name, and optionally a username, profile photo and bio. If you sign in with Apple or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from them.

Health and body information: your date of birth (which also gives your age), sex, height, weight, activity level, weight goal and the daily energy and nutrient targets you set, and any weight you log. This is sensitive information under Australian privacy law; we collect it only with your consent, to work out and track your targets.

Your food diary and plans: foods, meals and recipes you log or save, amounts, exercise, water, coffee, custom nutrients, meal prep plans and your shopping list.

Things you post and share: posts, photos, comments, likes ("Yummies"), who you follow, and reports you make.

Food data you contribute: products, nutrition label photos, corrections and "correct / incorrect" checks you add to Prepza’s shared food database.

Subscriptions: if you subscribe to Prepza Premium, you pay Apple through the App Store or Google through Google Play, and we never see your card or payment details. The store tells our subscription provider, RevenueCat, which plan you bought, when, the price, and when it renews, ends or is refunded. RevenueCat links this to your Prepza account so Premium unlocks on your devices. If you enter a creator code, we record which creator referred you so we can pay them a reward; we don’t give the creator your diary, health information or contact details.

Launch updates: if you sign up on prepza.com.au, we keep your email address to tell you when Prepza launches and to send occasional news about it. Every email lets you unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Information collected when you use features

Voice logging: your recording is sent to a speech-to-text service (Groq) to turn it into text, then discarded. We keep the text only as the food entries you choose to log.

Photo and text logging, label reading and recipe import: the photo, text or web page you provide is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to identify foods or read nutrition information. The results are shown to you to review before anything is saved.

AI Coach, Recipe Finder and restaurant food lookup: for the weekly AI Coach check-in, a summary of your week (energy and nutrient totals against your targets, your weight trend and your most-logged foods) is sent to Anthropic to write the check-in, which we save so you can read it again. Recipe Finder sends the description you type, and restaurant food lookup sends your search words, to Anthropic, which searches recipe or restaurant websites. The foods you ask Recipe Finder to avoid are checked on your phone and not sent. Product corrections you suggest may also be checked by Anthropic before they’re added to the shared database.

Barcode scans and food searches: the barcode or search words are sent to food databases (Open Food Facts directly from your device, and FatSecret through our servers) to find matching products.

Apple Health: if you turn it on, Prepza reads your active energy and your weigh-ins (body mass) from Apple Health on your iPhone, and saves them to your diary so they count towards your day and your weight progress. Prepza never writes to Apple Health. You can turn this off in Settings or in the Health app at any time. We don’t use Apple Health information for advertising or marketing, don’t sell it, and don’t share it with anyone except the service providers that store it for us and a coach you choose to share your diary with.

Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device’s push token so we can send you notifications (for example about activity on your posts or notes from your coach) through Expo’s push service. Reminders you set are scheduled on your phone. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in your phone’s settings.

Crash and error reports: when Prepza crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix it. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the code, the app version, your device model and operating system, and the app’s recent steps leading up to it (for example which screen was open and which server requests it made, without their search words or ids). Prepza doesn’t send your user ID, name, email address, IP address, screenshots or anything you type. Sentry stores reports in the European Union (Germany) and deletes them after at most 90 days.

Shopping: when you open Woolworths or Coles inside Prepza, you are using their website. Anything you sign in with or add there goes to them under their own privacy policies; Prepza doesn’t see your login or your order.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, sell your information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

How we use your information

To run Prepza: calculate your targets, keep your diary, plans and settings, and sync them across your devices.

To run the social features you choose to use: show your posts to people you share them with, and send you notifications about activity on them.

To run Premium: check your subscription, unlock Premium features and pay creators who referred subscribers.

To keep the shared food database accurate and the community safe: including reviewing reports and removing content or accounts that break our Terms of Use.

To provide support, keep Prepza secure and fix problems, including from crash and error reports.

What other people can see

Your diary, health information and targets are private to you. Your name, username, profile photo, bio, posts, comments and likes are visible to other Prepza users. When you share a meal you can choose whether its ingredients are shown.

If you choose to share your diary with a coach or personal trainer (Settings → Coach or PT), they can see your food diary, targets, exercise (including what Apple Health sends to Prepza), water, coffee and weight, and leave you notes. They can’t change anything. Sharing only starts when you give them a code, and you can stop it at any time; they lose access straight away. Coaches must use what they see only to coach you.

Products and corrections you add to the shared food database are visible to everyone and stay available after you delete your account, no longer linked to you.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of Prepza for us, and only what they need: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Anthropic (AI reading of photos, labels, text and recipes, the AI Coach, Recipe Finder and restaurant food lookup), Groq (speech-to-text for voice logging), FatSecret and Open Food Facts (food databases), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Sentry (crash and error reports), Expo (app delivery, updates and push notifications), Apple (the App Store, in-app purchases, Apple Health and Sign in with Apple), and Google and Meta if you use their sign-in or app stores.

We also share your diary with a coach or personal trainer if you choose to (see above).

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States and the European Union. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account & data. This permanently deletes your account, diary, health information, posts, comments, photos, coach sharing and settings. Backups may keep copies for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Records of subscription payments are kept, no longer linked to your account, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Crash and error reports are deleted by Sentry after at most 90 days.

Foods and products you added to the shared database stay for others to use, but are no longer linked to you.

Your choices and rights

You can see and correct most of your information directly in the app. You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

Prepza is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Everyone gives their date of birth before using Prepza; if it shows they are under 13, they cannot continue and the date is not saved. Your date of birth is never shown to anyone else. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and access to it is restricted, so only you (and our service providers acting for us) can reach your private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if how we handle information changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.

Questions or requests: support@prepza.com.au.
